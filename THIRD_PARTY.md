# Third-party licenses

The texts, and the list of what ships in the binary and in app components, are in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES` (generated by `scripts/notices.py`; CONTRIBUTING.md says how to regenerate it). The default binary links 233 crates. `rusqlite` and its bundled SQLite are only in a `--features lab` build, not in a release.

The scan below was taken over the whole host workspace, features on.

Checked with `cargo metadata --all-features` over the host workspace (280 packages: `spared` and 279 dependencies; 272 without the `lab` feature). Everything is permissive: MIT, Apache-2.0 (including with the LLVM exception), BSD-3-Clause, ISC, Unicode-3.0, Unlicense, Zlib.

Notes:
- `webpki-roots` is CDLA-Permissive-2.0 (Mozilla root certificate data). It is redistributed with the host binary; keep this attribution.
- Two packages are `GPL-2.0-only OR BSD-3-Clause`; spared uses them under BSD-3-Clause.
- `foldhash` (via `hashbrown`, wasmtime) is Zlib. `r-efi` offers `LGPL-2.1-or-later` as a third option; spared uses it under MIT OR Apache-2.0.
- SQLite is in the public domain and is compiled into guest components through `libsqlite3-sys` (MIT).
- Guest crates under `apps/` and `probes/` are separate workspaces and were not part of this scan. Their direct dependencies (`wstd`, `rusqlite`, `serde`) are MIT or Apache-2.0.

Rerun after dependency changes: `cargo metadata --format-version 1` and list the `license` fields.
